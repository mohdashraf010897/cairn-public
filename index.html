Verifiable Privacy Architecture

What happens on YouTube stays inside your browser.

Inspect the Cairn extension in Chrome DevTools → Network at any time: on youtube.com, Cairn initiates zero requests to Cairn or third-party servers. On this landing page, only optional votes/signups and cookie-free button counts use our first-party endpoint. Read the full Privacy Policy.

What Data Exists Where It Lives Leaves Your Device?
Typed session intentions & 7-day viewing stats chrome.storage.local on your computer Never. Stays strictly on this device and clears automatically when Cairn is removed (synced preferences reset anytime in Settings).
Video title & channel quality scoring Evaluated locally on youtube.com (cached in chrome.storage.local) Never. Deterministic local rules—zero external AI or analytics endpoints.
Focus preferences, schedule & optional SHA-256 Lock PIN hash chrome.storage.sync No Cairn servers. Syncs across your signed-in Chrome browsers only if you enable Google Chrome Sync.
Does Cairn block YouTube completely or break embedded tutorial videos on other sites?
Neither. Cairn runs only on desktop youtube.com pages—embedded docs videos on Notion, Coursera, or internal wikis are untouched, and YouTube Search stays completely accessible whenever you need a tutorial.
Why are off-goal or clickbait videos marked with a quiet grey badge instead of bright red?
Bright red warning badges act like neon signs—psychologically triggering curiosity ("forbidden-fruit effect"). Cairn highlights goal matches in calm Alpine Pine (▲ Matches goal) and lets low-signal items fade quietly into the background.
Does Cairn work on Brave, Microsoft Edge, or Arc?
Yes. Any desktop Chromium browser that supports Manifest V3 Chrome extensions runs Cairn with identical local storage and zero-server guarantees.
